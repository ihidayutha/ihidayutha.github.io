Kaliningrad Kwanglim Church 

The hometown of philosopher Immanuel Kant, it was once German territory but became Russian territory after World War II. Despite being separated from mainland Russia, descendants of Koreans forcibly relocated by the Soviet Union still live here. Around 15 people began gathering for worship, and in 2010, at the request of the Russian Methodist Church (RUMC) and with the support of the Kwanglim Church Women's Missionary Society, the Kaliningrad Kwanglim Church was built. The church's growth was so great that even in the cold winter, the doors were kept open and services held outside the sanctuary. In 2018, with the support of the Kwanglim Women's Missionary Society, the sanctuary was expanded.
